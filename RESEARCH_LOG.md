## Layout & Concept Research

* **[Modern Office on Wheels Concept](https://outsourcing-today.ro/?p=9721)** - Design concepts for mobile professional workspaces and interior lighting integration.
* **[Mobile Fablabs Examples (Marcello Tania)](https://marcellotania.com/mobileFablabs.html)** - Archive of mobile makerspace designs, equipment selection, and workshop layouts.
* **[FabLab Nomads](https://www.fablabs.io/labs/FabLabNomads)** - Documentation and network data for mobile hardware laboratories.
* **[Adventure Wagon Configurator](https://adventurewagon.com/pages/configurator)** - Modular interior systems and standardized vehicle mounting configurations.

## Structural & Aluminum Framing (30/30 & 80/20)

* **[How to Fasten 80/20 to Your Van (The Wanderful)](https://thewanderful.co/blog/how-to-fasten-extruded-aluminum-8020-to-your-van)** - Mechanical hardware and methods for securing aluminum profiles to a vehicle chassis.
* **[Engineers Who Vanlife: Extruded Aluminum Build Guide](https://engineerswhovanlife.com/extruded-aluminum-van-build-guide/)** - Engineering specifications for T-slot aluminum and comparisons with traditional framing materials.
* **[Custom Van Fit-out Using T-Slot Aluminum (GAP Engineering)](https://www.gapengineering.com.au/blog/custom-van-fit-out-using-t-slot-aluminium-extrusions/)** - Technical applications of T-slot extrusions in high-end vehicle cabinetry.
* **[GAP Engineering: Design & Fabrication Services](https://gapengineering.com.au/services/design-and-fabrication/)** - Standards for professional-grade T-slot assembly and fabrication.
* **[Van-der-moon Moon Frame](https://nomadicsupply.com/van-der-moon-moon-frame-aluminum-camper-van-framing-crossbar/)** - Aluminum crossbar systems for overhead storage and structural framing.
* **[Aluminum Framing & Cabinetry (Reddit)](https://www.reddit.com/r/vandwellers/comments/16v3xv5/aluminum_framing_completed_cabinetry_and/)** - Technical discussion on fastener selection and rattle-prevention in mobile aluminum frames.
* **[YouTube: How to Build an 8020 Van](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fhQh9AQInew)** - Practical assembly techniques for T-slot aluminum systems.
* **[YouTube: Aluminum Framing for Van Conversion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XqiroMdqZ0Y)** - Workflow for skeletal framing and profile alignment in a van interior.
